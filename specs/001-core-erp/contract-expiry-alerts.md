# Contract Expiry Alert Requirements

- Alert must be triggered 8 hours before midnight on the day of contract expiry.
- Alert method: Popup notification in UI (configurable per user), and optional email alert.
- Alert timing and method must be user-configurable (default: 8 hours before midnight).
- Alert must include: Contract No, Party, Expiry Date, Days Remaining, Action Link.
- Admins can override alert window per contract or globally.
